# Pricing Review — June 2026

## Current Production Configuration

* Delivery fee: 7 LYD
* Driver payout: 7 LYD
* Delivery margin charged in vendor settlement: 3 LYD
* Platform max radius: 15 km
* Per-km fee: 0 LYD

## Market Observations

* Pharmacies commonly charge 5–7 LYD delivery
* Pharmacies commonly deliver up to 15 km
* Customers expect simple fixed pricing
* Long-distance deliveries reduce profitability
