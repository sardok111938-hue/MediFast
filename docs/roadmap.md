# Product Principles

1. Audit before implementation.
2. Server-side enforcement over client-side logic.
3. Single source of truth for all business data.
4. Keep MVP simple.
5. Production-safe migrations only.
6. Optimize operations before adding growth features.
7. Measure everything before automating decisions.
